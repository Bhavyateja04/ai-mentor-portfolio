## Student-AI Scenario Risk Classification (EU AI Act Tiers)

| # | Scenario | Tier | Reasoning |
|---|----------|------|-----------|
| 1 | AI résumé editing (bullets, grammar polish) | **Limited** | Allowed; student must disclose AI assistance to recruiters on request. |
| 2 | AI mock interviews (ChatGPT practice) | **Minimal** | Free use. Practice tool only; no recruiter or third party is affected. |
| 3 | AI-written application essays | **High-risk** | Allowed only with mentor review + signed student declaration before submission. |
| 4 | AI-coded GitHub portfolio projects | **High-risk** | Allowed with mandatory oral defence; student must walk through every accepted line. |
| 5 | AI in graded internal assignments | **Unacceptable / Limited** | Unacceptable where ability is tested. Limited (with declaration) where application is tested. |
| 6 | AI-generated CGPA on résumé | **Unacceptable** | Falsification of academic record. Banned outright. |
| 7 | AI-cloned voice for video interview | **Unacceptable** | Identity fabrication. Banned outright. Recruiters increasingly require live segments. |
| 8 | AI for skills self-assessment | **Minimal** | Free use. Self-reflection tool; no third party harmed. |

---

## Permitted Uses

1. AI **may** be used to: rewrite résumé bullets for clarity and grammar.
2. AI **may** be used to: simulate mock interviews for personal practice.
3. AI **may** be used to: explain technical concepts (DSA, OS, DBMS) in study mode.
4. AI **may** be used to: generate placement-prep study schedules from a given syllabus.
5. AI **may** be used to: produce one-page company-prep briefs (when verified against the company's official website).
6. AI **may** be used to: review and suggest improvements to student-written code (with subsequent oral defence).

---

## Prohibited Uses

1. AI may **NOT** be used to: fabricate projects, internships, or experience the student did not complete.
   **Detection:** Mentor oral defence — student must explain technical decisions and walk through project code.

2. AI may **NOT** be used to: clone the student's voice or likeness for asynchronous video interviews.
   **Detection:** Recruiters require live-segment verification; voice-continuity anomalies flagged by T&P cell.

3. AI may **NOT** be used to: generate or alter academic records (CGPA, marks, transcripts).
   **Detection:** All CGPA/marks claims cross-referenced with the college academic portal before submission.

4. AI may **NOT** be used to: write essays where the student's voice is the assessment criterion (SOP, motivation letters).
   **Detection:** Mentor reviews 2–3 historical writing samples and compares voice consistency before sign-off.

---

## Enforcement

The placement cell will conduct random oral defences on portfolio projects, sampling 20% of placed students each cycle. Any unverifiable AI use that violates the Prohibited Uses above results in: first violation — written warning and portfolio re-defence within 7 days; second violation — placement support withdrawn for the current recruitment cycle. The placement cell acknowledges that not all violations are detectable; this policy relies in part on student integrity and the broader career consequences of misrepresentation discovered by a recruiter.
